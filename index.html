About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science (financial risk management track) at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China, supervised by Prof. Anthony Bellotti. My work sits at the intersection of document image intelligence and machine learning for financial risk: restoring and recognizing degraded document images, applying LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation to complex documents, and building credit-risk scoring models on multi-source heterogeneous data.

Research Interests

  • Document Intelligence and Computer Vision: restoration, enhancement, and OCR legibility of degraded documents; deep-learning and generative models for text-image reconstruction, leveraging priors such as text structure, layout redundancy, and text-image sparsity; efficient inference.
  • LLM Applications and Agent Workflows: collaboration between VLMs and LLMs on complex tasks; tool calling, task planning, and multi-step workflow orchestration (e.g. LangGraph) for information extraction, analysis, and decision support.
  • Multimodal RAG and Knowledge Conflicts: representation, indexing, and use of multimodal information in RAG; knowledge conflicts between parametric and retrieved knowledge; conflict detection, evidence provenance, and retrieval-side optimization; estimation and characterization of LLMs’ internal parametric knowledge.
  • Credit Risk and Financial Risk Modeling: personal and corporate credit scoring, default and fraud detection, and interpretable risk prediction over multi-source heterogeneous government and financial data.

Projects

Ningbo Personal Credit Scoring System (Credit Risk Assessment for Banks) 2023 – 2024
Research Assistant / Technical Lead · Ningbo Finance and Regulatory Bureau × Ningbo Big Data Bureau × UNNC
  • Supported by the Ningbo “Fan 3315 Plan”, the Ningbo Finance and Regulatory Bureau, and the Ningbo Big Data Bureau; aimed at providing banks and other financial institutions with more accurate credit risk assessments and key predictive factors.
  • Developed a personal credit risk scoring model on multi-department government data; cleaned, integrated, and engineered features from heterogeneous sources (social insurance, housing provident fund, medical insurance, public security, taxation, market supervision); built static features via encoding/binning and sequential features via RFM principles.
  • Defined risk entities by risk behaviors (housing provident fund loan delinquency; dishonest judgment debtor) to form supervised labels; a gradient boosting classifier achieved AUC 0.9179, significantly outperforming the baseline.
  • Results were anonymized, reviewed, and published to an external database queryable through an API.
