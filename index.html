Biography

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science (financial risk management track) at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China, supervised by Prof. Anthony Bellotti. My research sits at the intersection of document image intelligence and machine learning for financial risk: restoring and recognizing degraded document images, applying LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation to complex documents, and building credit-risk scoring models on multi-source heterogeneous data.

I hold an MSc in Financial Computing (Distinction) from Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and a BSc in Information and Computing Science from NingboTech University. Alongside research I have several years of experience as a university teaching assistant in machine learning and data analytics.

Research Interests

Document Intelligence & Vision

Restoration, enhancement and OCR legibility of degraded documents; deep-learning and generative models for text-image reconstruction, leveraging text-structure, layout-redundancy and sparsity priors; efficient inference.

LLM Applications & Agents

VLM–LLM collaboration on complex tasks; tool calling, task planning and multi-step workflow orchestration (e.g. LangGraph) for information extraction, analysis and decision support.

Multimodal RAG

Representation, indexing and use of multimodal information in RAG; knowledge conflicts between parametric and retrieved knowledge; conflict detection and evidence provenance.

Credit Risk Modeling

Personal and corporate credit scoring, default and fraud detection, and interpretable risk prediction over multi-source heterogeneous financial data.
